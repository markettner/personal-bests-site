Personal Bests

Personal Bests helps you discover your fastest efforts hidden inside your workouts.

🏃 All Workout Types 📊 Personal Records 🔒 Privacy First ⚡ On-Device Processing
🔍

Finds efforts you never logged

Most apps only show totals. Personal Bests scans every workout in Apple Health and uncovers your fastest segment of any length — even a blazing 1-mile buried in the middle of a long run.

🏅

Rank any distance, any timeframe

Track exactly the distances you care about — 400m, 5K, marathon, anything — across running, cycling, swimming, rowing and more. See where each effort ranks all-time, this year, or over a custom window.

🔒

Private, and everywhere you train

On iPhone, iPad and Mac with Home Screen widgets and personal-best notifications. Analysis runs on-device and syncs through your own iCloud — no third-party accounts, ever.

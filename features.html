01 Discover

Finds the bests you didn’t know you had

Most apps only show totals. Personal Bests checks every stretch of every workout in Apple Health and finds your fastest segment for each distance you care about.

  • Your fastest 5K might be kilometres 7 to 12 of a half marathon — it still counts
  • Your whole history, from Apple Watch, iPhone and other apps that write to Apple Health
  • New workouts are analysed automatically, even in the background
02 Rank

Any distance, any timeframe

Every segment becomes a leaderboard with exactly one competitor: you. You pick the distances and the time window you’re measured against — coming back from an injury, rank against the past three months instead of your all-time best.

  • 400 m, 5K, marathon — or any distance in metres, kilometres or miles
  • All-time, the past year, or any window in days, weeks, months or years
  • Indoor and outdoor ranked separately, if you prefer
  • Earned medals can stay yours, even after you beat them
03 Map New

See where you were fastest

Open a workout and your fastest segments are drawn on the route, each in its own colour. Shorter efforts sit on top of longer ones, so your fastest 5K stays visible inside your fastest 20K.

  • Tap a segment to show or hide it, or touch and hold to see just that one
  • Pauses and recording gaps aren’t bridged with straight lines
  • Choose what opens by default: your favourite segment, all of them, or no map
04 Clean up New

No medals for the train ride home

Forgot to stop your watch? Personal Bests spots segments that are too fast to be true for you and flags the workout, so a train or car ride can’t take a podium place. Nothing changes until you decide.

  • Judged against your own history, so a fast descent isn’t flagged but a train is
  • Crop a workout to the part you actually did — your Health data is never changed
  • Remove GPS spikes with one tap, and undo any fix at any time
05 Recap New

While you were away

Come back after a day or more and Personal Bests sums up everything since your last visit: the medals you picked up, and how your training compares with the stretch before.

  • New medals, workouts, and time and distance per sport
  • Active days, your longest workout, and your near misses
  • Shown at most once a day, and only when there’s something new
06 Stay on top

Know the moment you set a record

Notifications and widgets put your bests where you already look.

  • A notification when a new workout earns a medal, for the timeframe and places you choose
  • Rankings widget in every size, from the Lock Screen to the new full page on iOS 27
  • Recent Bests widget: your newest medals, newest first

Everywhere you train

Your iPhone reads Apple Health and does the analysis. Your iPad and Mac show the results, synced through your own iCloud — no third-party accounts, ever.

In your pocket after every workout

Each new workout is analysed in the background, and you get a notification when it earns a medal. Widgets put your rankings and newest bests on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Room to dig into your history

Your workout list and its details side by side, with rankings and medals on the big screen. Everything arrives from your iPhone through iCloud.

Review your season on the big screen

The same workouts, rankings and medals on your Mac, with keyboard shortcuts to step through your workouts and widgets on your desktop.
